# Note: the backstage areas (/BACKSTAGE/, /QR/, /QRLIBRARY/) are kept out of
# search results via per-page "noindex" meta tags, NOT a Disallow rule here.
# Reasoning: a Disallow would (1) publicly enumerate these "hidden" paths in a
# file anyone can read, and (2) stop crawlers from ever reading the noindex tag,
# which can leave a bare URL in search results anyway. Allowing the crawl while
# serving noindex is the reliable way to keep them unlisted. The pages stay
# reachable by anyone who has the direct URL.
