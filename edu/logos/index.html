Edu · Three.js

Three logos, one lens.

The same render-pipeline tool from the camera & frustum page, but the objects are Schuck Data logos — the brand lockup plus two originals. Move them, spin them, push them past the clip walls, and watch the camera draw each one onto the fixed screen.

Drag a logo or the center of projection to move it; drag empty space to orbit; scroll or pinch to zoom. Each logo has its own position, rotation, and scale in the panel. The screen always shows the actual rendered frame.

The three marks

Brand lockup
The site logo rebuilt in 3D wireframe: the four-node rising chart — the real Schuck Data brandmark — riding over an extruded SCHUCK / DATA wordmark, with the letter stems doubling as bars, just like the flat logo.
Constellation
An original mark: a small node-and-edge graph — points joined by links — for connected, related data. A network you can turn over in your hand.
Growth bars
An original mark: a field of bars climbing toward a corner — a three-dimensional bar chart that reads as upward trend and growth.

Why bother

A logo is usually a flat, fixed thing. Dropping the marks into the pipeline turns them into real geometry you can interrogate: see how field of view squeezes the lockup, how moving a mark off-axis skews its projection, how the clip walls slice a logo in half. It's a brand asset and a lesson in the same window.
