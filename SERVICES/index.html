Services

The whole pipeline, end to end.

Schuck Data specializes in understanding, developing, and repairing each step of the information pipeline — from how data is collected to how it's finally understood. Whether your process has a crack in the foundation or just needs a better finish, we can help.

The pipeline

Good data work isn't one step — it's a chain, and a break anywhere degrades everything downstream. We work across all seven stages.

  • Sampling

    Statistical methodology that ensures your data is representative before collection ever begins.

  • Measurement

    Instrument design that captures what you actually need to know.

  • Data cleaning

    Outlier identification and correction, so your analysis starts on solid ground.

  • Databasing

    Normalization and architecture that make your data reliable and queryable.

  • Analytics

    Modeling and forecasting that turn structured data into answers.

  • Reporting

    Automated dashboards that replace manual, repeated work.

  • Presentation

    At-a-glance design that communicates findings without a decoder ring.

What we offer

Our services range from quick data health reports, to development and delivery of custom report automations, to helping your organization build its own data department using your existing staff. Our goal is to get things working for you — so you don't need us anymore.

Data Health Reports

A front-to-back assessment of your data management process. We tell you what you're doing well, where the gaps are, and how to better leverage the information you already have. A good place to start when you're not sure where to begin.

Report Automation & Dynamic Dashboards

We turn tedious, repeated manual reports into beautiful, automated visualizations — reclaiming hours of labor previously tied up in tedium. If your team is copy-pasting numbers into spreadsheets every week, this is for you.

Statistical Modeling & Forecasting

Regression, risk analysis, and scenario modeling — ways to put the data you already have to better use. We scope these engagements to your questions, not the other way around.

AI Readiness

Like all data, AI is garbage in, garbage out. We assess your organization's data front to back and get it clean, structured, and trustworthy enough to actually build on — before you invest in the models that depend on it.

Data Department Development

For organizations ready to build internal capability, we help you develop your own data function using your existing staff. The goal is to get things working so you don't need us anymore.

Retainer Packages

Short- and long-term retainers — much more affordable than a full-time in-house hire, with the flexibility to scale up or down as your needs change.

Data Sculptures (prestige)

Your organization's unique data signature, rendered as digital art for public display. Our most distinctive offering — equal parts analytics and artifact.
