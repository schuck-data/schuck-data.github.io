Homepage directions — Schuck Data

Four design directions, same content in each. Each shows the hero plus a small secondary section so you can sense how the design extends down the page. All use the agreed palette (Deep Ink Blue, light mode) and Inter typography — the variable here is composition and visual character, not color or font.

Direction 1

Refined Minimal

The current direction, but elevated. Stays calm and uncluttered, but adds composition: a subtle dot pattern fading into the page, asymmetric left-aligned hero, "currently available" status pill, two clear CTAs. Below the hero, a quiet services row in a soft-grey band so the visitor sees there's a real site to scroll. Reference: Linear, Vercel, Plausible. Lowest visual risk; highest restraint.

Direction 2

Split with Data Graphic

Two-column hero with a stylized dashboard on the right. The text side carries the message; the right side reinforces it visually with a mini-dashboard (KPI tile, bar chart, sparkline) rendered in the brand color. Costs nothing in performance (pure CSS, no images) but adds immediate visual context: visitors see what the firm produces, not just what it claims. Slightly higher visual commitment than Direction 1.

Direction 3

Bold Editorial

Full-bleed deep-navy hero with a subtle grid pattern fading out at top-right. Confident, brand-forward. The hero panel itself becomes the visual statement — large white type on saturated brand color reads as substantial and memorable. Lower-key services section in white below for contrast. Risk: needs strong content to live up to its own register; weak copy on a bold hero looks like a startup pretending.
